GEOMETRY HANDBOOK

Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Geometry Handbook is an offline educational geometry reference published by Oleg Korunov. This policy describes the mobile app distributed through Google Play and the App Store. The same policy is available inside the app, under About → Privacy.

No accounts, no tracking

The app does not create accounts and contains no advertising, analytics or tracking code. It does not send us personal information and does not contact our servers. The Android release does not request internet access at all.

What is stored on your device

Only your chosen language, your theme, the entries you star and your best Practice score are saved, in the app’s local settings. Numbers you type into a calculator, your answers to questions and the text you type into search are processed on the device, stay in memory while the app is running, and are not saved to any history.

The handbook works offline

The entries, drawings, translations and fonts are installed with the app. Search, the interactive drawings, the calculators, Practice and the cheat sheet all work without a network connection.

Device permissions

The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, files or location.

Stores and the operating system

Google Play, the App Store and your operating system handle installation, update and crash data under their own rules. That happens outside the app, and we do not receive that data.

Deleting your settings

To delete what the app has saved, clear the app’s data or uninstall it. In the Android release, settings, favorites and the Practice score are excluded from cloud backup and from transfer to a new device, so a reinstall starts from scratch. On iOS, local preferences may be included in the device backup that your system manages.

Children

The app is a study aid. It collects nothing from anyone, including children, and shows no advertising.

Contacting us

If you write to us by email, we receive your address and the contents of your message and use them only to reply. Please do not send passwords or other sensitive information. Delivery is handled by your email provider and ours; this is separate from using the offline app.

Policy changes

Any change to the app’s data practices will be reflected here with an updated date.
